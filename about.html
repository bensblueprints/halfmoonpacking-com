Our Story

A Family Tradition in the Rockies

Halfmoon Packing & Outfitting was founded in 1998 with a simple mission: share the beauty of the Colorado Rockies with visitors from around the world. What began as a one-man guiding operation has grown into a full-service outfitter offering horseback riding, backcountry camping, guided hunting, and alpine fishing.

Located in Leadville, Colorado — the highest incorporated city in North America — we operate on thousands of acres of private and public land, including access to some of the most remote and pristine wilderness in the state.

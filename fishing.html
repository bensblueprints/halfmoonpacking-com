High Alpine Lakes

Come fish for Brooke and Brown Trout in one of the high mountain lakes in either the Mount Massive or Holy Cross Wilderness areas. Enjoy a true western experience with a short horseback ride to a glacial lake where you can catch lunch before riding home the same day. If you prefer a longer trip please check out our fishing trips in the camping section.

Floating Lake Trips

If you are after larger fish try catching Lake trout in either Turquoise or Twin Lakes. We will put you with an experienced guide in a large yacht (just kidding, its a small aluminum bass boat.) An all day fishing trip includes lunch and beverages.

EAGLE RIVER

Fish the head waters of the Eagle River as it begins its journey from the White River National Forest to the Colorado River. Your guide will assist you in selecting the fly that matches the current insect activity over the river. He will make sure that you get that fly where you want it as well.

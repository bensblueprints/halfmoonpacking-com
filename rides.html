Horseback Riding and Trail Riding

Scroll

If the current weather pattern continues we anticipate a soft opening around May 1. Please call for reservations. Come join us as the snow melts and the mountains take on their green hues. There is no better opportunity to view wildlife like deer, elk and moose.

Halfmoon Packing & Outfitting is located north of Leadville , Colorado off of Highway 24. Halfmoon Packing & Outfitting offers the best rides near Vail, Avon and Breckenridge Colorado. All rides have incredible mountain views and pass old mining or logging sites and cabins. We offer horseback rides daily from May 13, 2023 depending on snow depth. Rides can be booked by following the links below or by calling 719 486 4570.

We offer rides to all people 6 years old and older. We have limited capability for riders over 300 pounds.

Full Day Horseback Rides

Spend an entire day along a section of the Colorado Trail. The ride will take you through dark timber, aspen groves and open fields as it climbs above timberline and 12,000 feet overlooking the valley below.

Hourly to Half Day Horseback Rides

One hour to half day horseback rides in Colorado depart from the stables and gain up to 1,300 feet in elevation. From the top of Mt Zion you sill see a panoramic view encompassing Leadville, Twin Lakes and Turquoise Lake. This ride will take three and a half to four hours depending on how fast you travel and how many pictures you take!

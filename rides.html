Experience the Rockies on Horseback

There is no better way to experience the breathtaking beauty of the Colorado Rockies than from the saddle. Our guided horseback rides cater to all skill levels, from first-time riders to seasoned equestrians. With over 25 years of experience, our guides know every trail, meadow, and mountain vista in the region.

Our Rides

Hourly & Half-Day Rides

Perfect for families and beginners. Explore scenic trails with gentle, well-trained horses. Ages 6 and up.

$85 per person / hour

Full-Day Rides

Pack a lunch and spend the day in the backcountry. Visit alpine lakes, wildflower meadows, and historic mining sites.

$325 per person

Winter Rides

Experience the silent beauty of snow-covered trails. Heated lodge access before and after your ride.

$95 per person / hour

What to Expect

  • All rides include a safety briefing and basic riding instruction
  • Helmets provided (required for riders under 18)
  • Maximum group size of 8 riders per guide
  • Weight limit: 225 pounds (please notify us in advance if over 200 lbs)
  • Children ages 6-12 ride with a guide lead
  • Reservations recommended, especially weekends and holidays

Horse Camping

Backcountry Camping with Horse Support

Experience true wilderness camping without the heavy pack. Our pack horses carry your gear deep into the Colorado backcountry while you enjoy the ride. Wake up to alpine meadows, crystal-clear streams, and unobstructed mountain views.

Camping Options

Guided Horse Camping

Our guides lead you to pristine backcountry campsites, set up camp, prepare meals, and handle the horses. You simply enjoy the experience.

From $450/person/day

Summer Drop Camps

We pack your gear to a remote campsite and set up a base camp. You stay and explore at your own pace while we check in daily.

From $850/person (3-day minimum)

Pack Service

Have your own camp planned? We will pack your gear to your destination and return to pick it up when you are ready.

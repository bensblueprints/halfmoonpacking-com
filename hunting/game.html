BIG GAME HUNTING COLORADO

 If you missed the April 7 game deadline you are not completely in the dark. Colorado has replaced the Leftover Tag system with a secondary draw system, applications will be accepted form June 5, 2020 to July 7, 2020. Preference points can not be earned in the secondary draw. If you have any questions or need help give us a call 719 486-4570.

We offer big game hunts for antelope, bear, elk, moose, mule deer, and mountain lions. With hunting in three Game Management Units, Units 45, 48 and 49 we offer lots of space and opportunity. It is our goal to provide you with the hunt you are looking for. Check out the species pages to discover this year’s hunting season dates and the Preference Point requirements for non-resident hunters over the past few years. Colorado elk outfitters. Once you have chosen the species that you are after we will provide you with the type of hunt you are seeking. The Hunting Services section describes our products. They can easily be modified to meet your desires. Let one of our experienced guides lead you to the trophy you seek

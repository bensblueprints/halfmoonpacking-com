STAGECOACH

After 100 years Stage Coach service has returned to the town of Leadville! We offer 2.5 hour Stage Coach rides down Halfmoon Road.

In the nineteenth century Congress funded the development of transcontinental roads and stage service to improve mail delivery. These mail contracts always had a time limit for delivery. The time limits, usually around 25 days, required round the clock operation with coaches routinely arriving at their destination at all hours of the night. To commemorate these daunting cross country journeys we offer one hour Moonlight stage rides monthly on the night of the full moon..
